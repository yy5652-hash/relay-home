# Third-party notices

The look of the simulator page, the cards and the helper's page reuses four open-source projects. Nothing is loaded
from the network at run time: the values and the path data are copied into this repository's own files, which is why
their notices are kept here.

| What | Where it is used | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colour scales (slate, sky, amber, grass, tomato; light and dark) | `web/view.css`, `public/styles.css`, `public/reply.html` | [Radix Colors](https://github.com/radix-ui/colors) | MIT |
| Icons (path data) | `web/icons.js`, `public/index.html`, `public/reply.html` | [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide) | ISC |
| Easing curves | `public/styles.css`, `web/view.css` | [Open Props](https://github.com/argyleink/open-props) | MIT |
| The iPhone 14 Pro frame around the helper's page | `public/styles.css` | [Devices.css](https://github.com/picturepan2/devices.css) 0.2.0 | MIT |

What was changed: the colour steps are mapped onto the MCP Apps host style variables (`--color-background-primary`
and the rest) with the Radix values as the fallback; the icons are inlined as SVG that takes the colour of the text
around it; of Devices.css only the iPhone 14 Pro rules are kept, scaled down to sit beside the display.
