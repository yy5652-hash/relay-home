# Relay Home media

The demo film of Relay Home 2.2 and its captions. The source of the project is on the `main` branch.

- `Relay_Home_Demo.mp4` (2:05, 1920x1080)
- `Relay_Home_Demo.srt`

Live action: stock footage from Mixkit under the Mixkit Stock Video Free License (hourglass 28901, subway platform 1622, dominoes 5246, hand and phone 144).
Narration: synthetic voice (Kokoro, Apache-2.0). Music and sound: synthesised for this film.
